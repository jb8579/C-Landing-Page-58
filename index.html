Play Weekday Windfall

Getting started with Weekday Windfall is easy! A standard ticket includes 6 numbers per game line, giving you one chance to win with each line you play. To take home the Division 1 prize, simply match all 6 of your numbers with the 6 drawn in any order. Frequently Asked Questions

What is Winner Lotto?

Winner Lotto is an online lottery platform that gives players the chance to participate in Australia’s most exciting weekday draws. With guaranteed Division 1 prizes, it's designed to make winning more frequent and accessible.

How do I play Winner Lotto?

To play, simply choose 6 numbers from 1 to 45. You can pick your own numbers or use the QuickPick feature for random selection. Purchase at least 4 lines to get started. Draws are held every Monday, Wednesday, and Friday.

Can I play if I’m outside Australia?

Currently, Winner Lotto is only available for residents within Australia. Please check your local laws and our terms of service for eligibility before playing from another region.

How are prizes paid out?

If you win, your prize will be credited directly to your registered account. For larger winnings, additional identity verification may be required before the funds are released.
