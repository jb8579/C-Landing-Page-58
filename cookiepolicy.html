What are cookies?

Cookies are small data files stored on your device (computer, tablet, or smartphone) when you visit a website. These files allow websites to recognize your device and remember information about your visit, such as language preferences, login information, and browsing behavior. Cookies play an essential role in helping us deliver a smooth, tailored, and secure user experience.

Why do we use cookies?

We use cookies to:

Essential cookies: Required for the core functionality of mediapleasure.com, including page navigation, secure login, and access to key website features.
Analytical and performance cookies: Help us understand how visitors interact with our website, allowing us to improve content, user experience, and system performance.
Functional cookies: Remember your settings and preferences such as language or region, offering a personalized experience.
Advertising cookies: Enable the delivery of relevant ads and track their effectiveness through analytics and third-party ad partners.

What types of cookies do we use?

Our website uses the following types of cookies:
Session cookies: These are temporary and are automatically deleted when you close your browser. They help with temporary data storage during your visit.
Persistent cookies: These remain on your device for a specified period or until manually deleted. They are used to remember login details and preferences.
First-party cookies: These are created and used directly by mediapleasure.com to provide site functionality and store your preferences.
Third-party cookies: These are set by external services integrated into our website, such as Google Analytics or advertising partners, to track performance, gather data, and enhance marketing efforts.
